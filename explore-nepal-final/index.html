Where to go

Kathmandu

Seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites sit inside the valley, from Pashupatinath to Boudhanath stupa and Durbar Square.

Best: Oct to Nov

Pokhara

A lakeside town under the Annapurna range. Go boating on Phewa Lake or paraglide over the valley.

Best: Sep to May

Chitwan National Park

Jungle safaris to see one-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tigers and over 500 bird species.

Best: Oct to Mar

Lumbini

The birthplace of Gautama Buddha, with the Maya Devi Temple and monasteries built by countries around the world.

Best: Nov to Feb

Everest Base Camp

A 12 to 14 day trek through Sherpa villages to the foot of Earth’s highest mountain, at 5,364 m.

Best: Mar to May

Annapurna Circuit

A classic trek crossing Thorong La pass at 5,416 m, with changing landscapes from rice terraces to high desert.

Best: Oct to Nov

Home of the Himalayas

Eight of the 10 tallest mountains in the world.
