About Us

Explore Nepal is a student-built travel guide created to help visitors discover the country’s mountains, heritage and people. We believe the best trips begin with clear, honest information written by people who know the place.

Our guides cover popular routes like Everest Base Camp and quieter corners like the Tharu villages near Chitwan. We encourage responsible tourism: carry out your waste, hire local guides and stay in family-run lodges so the money stays in the community.

Whether you have a weekend or a month, we want you to leave with stories worth retelling.

Our mission

Make planning a Nepal trip simple, safe and respectful.

Our values

Local first, honest advice, low impact on nature and culture.

Our team

A small group of students passionate about web design and travel.
